# Release notes · 0.2.0-beta.1

First public beta of Baggler for WoW Forever (client 1.60.1.71037).

## What's included

- Customizable bags and personal/account bank, with keyboard/mouse and controller support.
- Automatic and custom categories, folding sections, search filters, favorites and sorting.
- Item cards and equipment comparisons.
- Item transfers with native bank, mail, trade and merchant windows.
- Bag equip/replace and stack splitting from the item menu.
- Remappable controller buttons and full controller navigation in settings, with B to go back.
- 46 accent colours, including **My class** and every class colour.
- Labels for icon-only buttons when they have controller focus, as well as on mouse hover.

## Settings share codes

Settings share codes (`/baggler export` and `/baggler import`) use a new format in this release. Codes made with an earlier Baggler build can't be imported; export your settings again and share the new code.
